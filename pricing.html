How freelancers price their work

Your calculator number is the floor. Quotes usually sit higher.

1. Start from your number

Use the rate calculator, then add buffer for tax, sick days, and unpaid admin.

2. Pick a pricing style

  • Hourly — scope unclear
  • Fixed project — clear deliverable
  • Day rate — often ~6–7× hourly

3. Simple quote formula

Hours estimate × your rate × 1.2 (buffer) = quote. Write what’s included and what’s not.

4. Make money from this site later

Free tool → trust → email list or paid checklist. Or offer setup help on the hire page.
