How freelancers price their work

Your calculator gives a baseline. Real quotes usually sit a bit higher.

1. Start from your number

Use the free rate calculator, then add buffer for tax, sick days, and unpaid admin.

2. Price the job, not only the hour

  • Hourly — good when scope is unclear
  • Fixed project — good when the deliverable is clear
  • Day rate — common for consulting (often 6–7× hourly)

3. Simple quote formula

Hours estimate × your rate × 1.2 (buffer) = quote. Write what’s included and what’s not.

4. Beginner money tip

Ship useful free tools (like this site), collect emails later, then offer a paid checklist or template.
